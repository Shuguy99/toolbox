What is URL Encoding?

URL encoding (percent-encoding) replaces unsafe characters in URLs with a '%' followed by two hexadecimal digits. For example, a space becomes %20 and a Hebrew or Cyrillic character becomes %D0%A1. This keeps URLs valid for parsing by browsers and servers.
