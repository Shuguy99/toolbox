What is a Unix Timestamp?

The Unix timestamp is the number of seconds since January 1, 1970, 00:00:00 UTC. It's a universal time format used in APIs, databases (MySQL, PostgreSQL), and programming languages for storing and exchanging times without timezone ambiguity.
